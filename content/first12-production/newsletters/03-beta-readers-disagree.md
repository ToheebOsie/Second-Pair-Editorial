# Your beta readers disagree. Do not count votes yet.

**Second Pair Newsletter**  
**Canonical problem:** `/problems/beta-readers-disagree`

One reader says the opening is slow. Another says the atmosphere is what they liked. Before deciding who is right, check whether they are describing the same thing.

Put each comment beside the passage it refers to. “Slow” might mean too much explanation, a scene with no change, or a reader waiting for a question the story has not yet raised. “Confusing” might mean a missing clue or simply a mystery the reader has not solved yet.

Separate the reader's reaction from their explanation and from the fix they suggest. “I lost interest here” is not the same as “cut this scene”.

A majority can reveal a pattern, but one reader can still point out a real continuity problem. And several readers can share an expectation that does not fit the book you are trying to write.

**Try this:** Pick one disagreement and write down what each person actually experienced. Then reread the same passage before deciding what to change.
