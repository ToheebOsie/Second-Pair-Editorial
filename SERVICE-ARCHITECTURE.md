# Second Pair Editorial — Service Architecture v1

## Commercial principle

Second Pair Editorial is not built around selling the largest possible edit. It is built around identifying the manuscript's actual need and routing the author to the smallest sufficient service.

Core method:

**EXPERIENCE → EVIDENCE → PATTERN → DIAGNOSIS → PRIORITY → AUTHOR DECISION**

## Service ladder

### READ
**Alpha Reading** — developing manuscripts; asks whether the story is working while it is still taking shape.

**Beta Reading** — complete or substantially revised manuscripts; asks how the story actually lands on a reader.

### DIAGNOSE
**Manuscript Diagnostic** — for authors who know something is wrong but do not yet know what; separates symptoms from underlying problems and routes the next step.

### DEVELOP
**Developmental Editing** — deep story-level work on structure, plot, character, pacing, scene function, information flow, themes and revision strategy.

### REFINE
**Line Editing** — strengthens clarity, rhythm, voice and scene-level prose without flattening the author's style.

**Copy Editing** — corrects grammar, punctuation, usage and consistency after the story and prose are settled.

### POLISH
**Proofreading** — final quality-control pass after editing.

### REVISE
**Revision Review** — evaluates whether the author's revision solved the important problems and whether new issues appeared.

### COMPLETE
**Complete Manuscript Development** — coordinated multi-stage support for manuscripts that need an editorial path rather than a single service.

## Service boundaries

| If the author's question is... | Route |
|---|---|
| Is the developing story working? | Alpha Reading |
| How does the finished story feel to a reader? | Beta Reading |
| Something feels wrong, but I cannot identify it. | Manuscript Diagnostic |
| What needs to change in the story? | Developmental Editing |
| Can the writing be stronger without changing the story? | Line Editing |
| Is the manuscript technically correct and consistent? | Copy Editing |
| What errors remain before submission/publication? | Proofreading |
| Did my revision actually solve the problems? | Revision Review |
| I need several stages of editorial support. | Complete Manuscript Development |

## Premium-service standard

A Second Pair deliverable should distinguish:

1. **Reaction** — what the reader experienced.
2. **Evidence** — where the manuscript produced that experience.
3. **Pattern** — whether the issue repeats or is isolated.
4. **Diagnosis** — what the underlying problem may be.
5. **Priority** — what deserves attention first.
6. **Author decision** — the author remains responsible for the final creative choice.

## Inline-comment standard

Inline comments should support the manuscript-level diagnosis. Avoid comments that merely say "I like this," "confusing," or "slow" without explaining the useful part of the reaction. Use page-level comments as evidence for broader patterns.

## Sample-read standard

The sample read is a demonstration of quality, not a miniature free version of the full service. Recommended scope: 1–2 chapters, selected inline comments, concise overall reaction, 2–4 substantive observations and a short indication of what should be investigated next.

## Pricing rule

The corpus does not provide reliable enough pricing evidence to change published rates. Current public rates remain unchanged. Scope and deliverables should be refined from corpus intelligence; pricing should be validated separately against the professional market.
