# Second Pair — Client Welcome & Editorial Handoff

## Client-facing confirmation must establish

- what was agreed;
- which manuscript version is being used;
- what the client will receive;
- what is outside scope;
- when work starts;
- when delivery is expected;
- how questions should be sent.

## Internal handoff

The editor receives:

1. client's original question;
2. agreed service;
3. evidence supplied during intake;
4. previous reader/editor feedback;
5. revision history where relevant;
6. exact deliverables;
7. exclusions;
8. manuscript filename/version;
9. dates;
10. special handling notes.

## Non-negotiable

The commercial summary must never overwrite the editorial evidence. The manuscript remains the primary object of analysis.
