# Q06 — Why do my characters sound the same?

**Canonical page:** `/problems/characters-sound-the-same`  
**Tool:** No dedicated tool yet  
**Priority:** First inbound-authority cluster

## Reader problem
Characters do not need different catchphrases to sound different. Voice can come from what each person notices, what they value, how they react under pressure, and the rhythm of their thinking.

## Do not jump to the obvious fix
We would be careful about solving this with surface quirks. If the characters think and react in the same way, changing vocabulary will only put different clothes on the same voice.

## Five-question investigation
"Would the dialogue still feel distinct without dialogue tags?","Do characters react differently to the same event?","Does each character have a recognizable worldview or priority?","Are differences consistent without becoming caricature?","Is the underlying characterization distinct before changing the prose?"

## What professional investigation adds
We would look at the characters before I look at the word choices: what each person notices, wants, assumes, avoids, and how they respond to the same situation. Then the prose has something real to express.

## Professional lens
Voice, dialogue, diction, rhythm, viewpoint and differentiated character choices.
