# V5.18.8 — Pricing architecture update

## Approved direction
International English-language authors paying in USD; tiered entry services and a premium core; delivery by independent editors under the Second Pair Editorial brand; premium positioning with sustainable project flow.

## Scope-led starting fees

- Manuscript Diagnostic: from $750; final quote after scope review.
- Revision Review: from $500; final quote depends on the revision brief, manuscript change, and agreed coverage.
- Complete Manuscript Development: from $3,500; staged proposal defines included services, deliverables, and revision boundaries.

## Calculation rules

- Per-word services are priced as `max(word_count × rate, minimum_fee)`.
- Rates shown in the calculator are indicative estimates, not binding quotes.
- Scope-led services display a starting fee, not a calculated final price.
- Final price and turnaround are confirmed before work begins. Complexity, manuscript condition, deadline, and deliverables may affect the quote.
- Keep `config/services.ts` as the canonical source for rates and minimum fees. Do not duplicate numeric rates in page-specific components.

## Editor economics — planning assumption, not a contractual policy

The initial business model used a 60% editor / 40% business allocation as a planning hypothesis. It is not automatically the compensation policy. Validate each service against actual editor hours, target effective hourly compensation, payment fees, project management, quality control, rework exposure, taxes, and overhead. Increase a project quote when its scope cannot meet the agreed editor compensation floor and sustainable business contribution.

## Validation still required

The public prices are approved starting architecture, not proof of conversion or profitability. Track qualified enquiries, quotes, acceptance, actual editor hours, effective editor hourly pay, scope overruns, delivery quality, and repeat/referral work before making further price changes.
