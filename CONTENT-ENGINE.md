# Second Pair Editorial — Content Engine

## Goal

Turn the Author Problems Library into a steady stream of useful content without repeating the same article in five places.

## Source structure

Each content cycle starts with one real problem:

**PROBLEM → INSIGHT → EXAMPLE → PRACTICAL CHECK → RESOURCE → OPTIONAL SERVICE**

## Content formats

### Facebook
Use the problem as a conversation starter. Give a useful observation and invite authors to compare experiences.

### Instagram
Use one clear idea per carousel or short video. Lead with the author's actual question.

### TikTok
Start with the problem in spoken language: “If your middle feels slow, don't start cutting scenes yet.” Then explain one possible cause.

### LinkedIn
Use the same editorial idea but frame it around the craft, process or lesson rather than selling the service.

### Substack / The Second Pair
Go deeper. Explain the problem, show why the obvious diagnosis can be wrong, and give the author a way to test the idea.

### Community replies
Answer the question in the thread. Do not turn a helpful reply into an advertisement.

## Example content chain

Problem: **“My opening feels slow.”**

- Facebook: Why an opening can feel slow even when something important is happening.
- Instagram: 5 things to check before cutting your first chapter.
- TikTok: “Your opening may not need more action. It may need a clearer reason to care.”
- LinkedIn: What editors can learn from reader attention in the first chapter.
- Substack: A full explanation of hook, context, stakes and reader questions.
- Community reply: A short answer with two things the author can check.
- Checklist: “Does the first chapter give the reader a reason to continue?”
- Sample-read question: “Where did your attention dip?”

## Quality check

Before publishing, ask:

- Does this answer a real author question?
- Is there a concrete example?
- Does it avoid presenting taste as a universal rule?
- Does it distinguish reader reaction from editorial judgment?
- Does it give the author something they can actually check?
- Is the language normal and specific?
- Is the service mention earned by the topic?
