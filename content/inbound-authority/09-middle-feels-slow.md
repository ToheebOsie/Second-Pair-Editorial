# Q09 — Why does my middle feel slow when every scene seems necessary?

**Canonical page:** `/problems/middle-feels-slow`  
**Tool:** /tools/reader-friction  
**Priority:** First inbound-authority cluster

## Reader problem
A scene can matter and still make the middle feel slow. Sometimes several scenes are doing slightly different versions of the same job, so the story keeps moving sideways instead of forward.

## Do not jump to the obvious fix
“Every scene is necessary” is not quite the same as saying every scene needs to stay separate. Several good scenes can still create a slow stretch when they keep producing the same kind of movement.

## Five-question investigation
"What unique work does each scene perform?","Does the situation materially change?","Does a choice create a consequence that carries forward?","Are several scenes delivering the same information or emotional beat?","Can scenes be combined without losing a distinct function?"

## What professional investigation adds
We would look for repeated scene functions, delayed consequences, and places where a choice does not seem to change what comes next. Only then would we start talking about cuts or compression.

## Professional lens
Progression, scene function, escalation, repeated beats, consequences and the difference between necessary material and necessary form.
