# Second Pair — Inbound Measurement Specification

## Primary objective

Measure qualified self-selection and paid outcomes, not vanity traffic.

## Funnel events

1. Organic/search discovery.
2. Problem-page visit.
3. Problem-page engagement.
4. Tool/resource use.
5. Sample request.
6. Private inquiry.
7. Qualified inquiry.
8. Proposal sent.
9. Paid project.
10. Average project value.
11. Repeat project.
12. Referral.

## Required dimensions

- entry problem;
- landing page;
- content cluster;
- diagnostic tool used;
- service route recommended;
- source/referral;
- project value band;
- repeat/referral outcome.

## Commercial questions

- Which manuscript problems attract qualified authors?
- Which pages produce inquiries rather than only visits?
- Which tools lead to serious inquiries?
- Which problem categories lead to premium diagnostic work?
- Which routes produce repeat work or referrals?

## Guardrail

Do not optimize for traffic alone. A smaller amount of highly qualified problem-led discovery is more relevant to the business model than large generic editorial traffic.
