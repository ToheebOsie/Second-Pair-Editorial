# Second Pair Editorial — Client Email Templates

These are working templates. Keep them natural and adapt them to the actual conversation.

## 1. Sample received

Subject: We received your sample

Hi [Name],

Thanks for sending [Book Title]. We’ve received the sample and the information you included about what you want help with.

We’ll take a look at the pages and get back to you by email. The point of the sample is to give you a useful sense of how we read and comment on a manuscript, and to help us see whether we’re the right fit for what you need.

Best,
Second Pair Editorial

## 2. Need more information

Subject: A quick question about [Book Title]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for getting in touch. Before we suggest the best way to approach [Book Title], could you clarify [specific question]?

That will help us understand whether you need a reader's response, a closer diagnosis of the manuscript, or a more hands-on editing pass.

Best,
Second Pair Editorial

## 3. Sample response — reader-focused

Subject: Your sample read — [Book Title]

Hi [Name],

Thanks again for sending the sample. We’ve finished the read.

The main thing that stood out to us was [specific observation]. We saw this in [brief page/scene reference], where [brief evidence]. As a reader, the effect was [reader effect].

We think the most useful next step is [service or discussion], because the question you brought to us is mainly about [reason].

If you’d like to continue, we can outline the scope and price before you decide anything.

Best,
Second Pair Editorial

## 6. Revision review follow-up

Subject: How is the revision going?

Hi [Name],

I wanted to check in because you mentioned that you were revising [Book Title]. If you reach a point where you want another reader to look at whether the main changes worked, a Revision Review may be useful.

No pressure at all — if you’re still in the middle of the revision, keep going. The review makes more sense once you have a new draft you’re ready to test.

Best,
Second Pair Editorial

If anything was unclear or you expected something we didn’t cover, feel free to tell us. That helps us improve the service.
