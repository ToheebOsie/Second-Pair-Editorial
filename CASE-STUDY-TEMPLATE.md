# Second Pair — Anonymized Case Study Template

Use only with client permission or sufficiently anonymized material approved for publication.

## 1. Author concern

What the author believed was wrong, in their own language.

## 2. Context

Genre, manuscript stage, relevant revision history and prior feedback. Avoid identifying details unless permission explicitly covers them.

## 3. Investigation question

What did Second Pair need to find out?

## 4. Reader evidence

Specific reader reactions and manuscript locations supporting the investigation.

## 5. Pattern

What recurred across passages/scenes/feedback?

## 6. Diagnosis

What explanation best fit the evidence? Preserve alternative explanations where evidence remained incomplete.

## 7. Priority

What deserved attention first, and why?

## 8. Author decision

What revision or editorial decision did the author make? The author remains the final creative decision-maker.

## 9. Outcome

Only report an outcome that can be verified and that the client has approved for publication.

## 10. Evidence boundary

State what the case demonstrates and what it does not establish. One case is not population-level evidence.

## Publication rule

Never manufacture a case study from an internal example and present it as a client result. A method demonstration must be labelled as a demonstration.
