# When feedback becomes another thing you cannot get through

**Second Pair Newsletter**  
**Canonical problem:** `/problems/too-much-feedback`

If you have comments from six readers, an editor's report and notes from your writing group, it can become hard to see the manuscript for the feedback.

Do not turn every comment into a task. First separate what the reader felt, what they think caused it and what they want you to change. Those are three different things.

Then put comments about the same passage together. “The chapter drags”, “nothing changes” and “they have the same argument again” may all point to repeated scene function. A single note about a missing plot step may also deserve attention even if nobody else noticed it.

Keep a separate list of what readers liked, remembered or found affecting. You need to know what to protect as well as what to revise.

**Try this:** Choose the one concern that seems to affect the largest part of the reading experience. Start there, not with the easiest sentence to fix.
