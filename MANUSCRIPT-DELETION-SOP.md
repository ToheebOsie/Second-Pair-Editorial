# Manuscript completion and deletion procedure

## Public commitment
When the editorial work is complete, Second Pair Editorial confirms completion and deletion with the author, then deletes the manuscript and any copies downloaded or created for the work. Manuscript contents are not used, reproduced, published, or repurposed for another purpose without the owner's permission and approval.

## Completion checklist

1. **Confirm completion with the author.** Send the project-completion message and ask the author to confirm that the agreed work is complete and the manuscript may be deleted. Do not treat this as permission to reuse manuscript content.
2. **Identify all copies.** Check the editorial inbox and sent mail, local downloads, working folders, editor handoff locations, exported copies, and any approved third-party storage used for the project.
3. **Delete working copies.** Delete the manuscript file and downloaded/created working copies from locations the team controls. Where independent editors hold copies, confirm their deletion rather than assuming it happened.
4. **Handle email attachments.** Remove the manuscript attachment from relevant inbox/sent-message locations where the mail service permits it. If a service retains backups or does not permit immediate deletion, do not claim those provider-held copies were erased; disclose the actual retention limitation to the author and document the handling.
5. **Handle backups and third parties.** Follow the relevant provider's actual deletion/retention controls. Do not promise immediate deletion from provider backups unless that behaviour has been verified.
6. **Record completion without retaining manuscript content.** Record project reference, completion date, confirmation date, deletion date, locations checked, responsible person, and any exception. Do not put manuscript excerpts or sensitive content in the deletion log.
7. **Close exceptions honestly.** If a copy cannot be deleted or an editor/provider has not confirmed deletion, keep the task open and tell the author what remains and why. Do not mark the deletion complete prematurely.

## Important boundary
This checklist is an operating procedure, not proof that every mail provider or backup deletes data instantly. Before launch, the business owner should verify the actual capabilities and retention behaviour of the mailbox, email delivery provider, editors' devices, and any other services used in the workflow.
