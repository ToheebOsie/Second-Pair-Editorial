## Evidence boundaries

Record what the supplied material cannot establish. Examples include:

- A short excerpt cannot establish full-manuscript pacing or structure.
- One reader cannot establish a recurring reader pattern.
- A reader's suggested solution does not establish the cause of the reaction.
- A critique partner's craft interpretation is not the same thing as an independent reader response.
- A line-level sample cannot establish whole-manuscript structural readiness.
- Author/editor knowledge must not be attributed to a reader before the manuscript provides it.
- Hypothetical reader reactions must never be presented as actual beta-reader feedback.
