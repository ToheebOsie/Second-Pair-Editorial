# How do I know whether my revision actually worked?

**Second Pair Newsletter**  
**Canonical problem:** `/problems/revision-worked`

## The useful distinction

A revision can make a passage cleaner without solving the problem that caused the revision. The only reliable test is tied to the original concern.

## Try this on the manuscript

Before revising, write the intended change in reader terms: “I want the reader to understand X earlier,” or “I want this choice to feel less arbitrary.” After revising, reread only for that effect. Then check what the revision changed elsewhere.

## What not to assume

Do not judge the new version mainly by whether it sounds better. A smoother passage can still fail its intended purpose, while a stranger-looking passage may have fixed the actual problem.

## What a professional read adds



### A useful boundary

This kind of self-check can tell you what to investigate. It cannot reproduce a sustained outside read, because the outside reader does not carry your private knowledge of the manuscript. That is where professional reader intelligence becomes useful: not as a louder opinion, but as a disciplined account of what the page made available to someone who did not write it.
