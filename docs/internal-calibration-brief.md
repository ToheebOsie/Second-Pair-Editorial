## Evidence boundaries

Record what the supplied material cannot establish. Examples include:

- A short excerpt cannot establish full-manuscript pacing.
- One reader cannot establish a recurring pattern.
- A reader’s suggested solution does not establish the cause.
- A line-level sample cannot establish whole-manuscript structural readiness.
