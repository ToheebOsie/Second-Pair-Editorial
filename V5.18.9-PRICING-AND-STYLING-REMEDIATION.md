# V5.18.9 — Pricing Page & Styling Remediation

## Changes

- Rebuilt the pricing page around four clear service tiers: Reader Insight, Diagnosis & Revision, Editorial Craft, and Longer-term Support.
- Replaced the crowded multi-column price rows with responsive service cards that clearly separate service purpose, rate/starting fee, minimum fee, and service-page link.
- Added a more deliberate pricing-page hierarchy: introductory pricing principle, tiered service list, budget calculator, scope principles, and final inquiry CTA.
- Refined the calculator panel, fields, result display, spacing, borders, and responsive behavior.
- Fixed rate formatting so fractional per-word prices such as `$0.0045/word` are not rounded to a misleading `$0.01` or `$0.005` display. Rates now display up to four decimal places; project totals remain currency-formatted.
- Added pricing-specific responsive rules for tablet and mobile layouts while retaining the existing brand palette and typography.
- Updated the package version to 5.18.9.
