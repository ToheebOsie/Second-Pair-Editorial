# Phase 14 — Diagnostic Tools Completion

## Purpose
Finish the two public diagnostic tools so they function as useful, bounded thinking tools rather than unfinished placeholders.

## Reader Friction Diagnostic
- Five reader-experience dimensions.
- Multi-select interaction.
- Immediate, human-readable interpretation for each selected dimension.
- Direct links to relevant problem investigations.
- Clear distinction between a starting point and a manuscript diagnosis.
- Reset/clear action.
- Mobile-safe reading layout.

## Feedback Conflict Analyzer
- One-comment-per-line workflow.
- Client-side sorting into agreement, preference, expectation mismatch, evidence-needed and unresolved.
- Shows the original comments under each category.
- Explicitly avoids deciding which reader/editor is correct.
- Direct route to Feedback Reconciliation and disagreement guidance.
- Reset action.

## Commercial guardrails
The tools deliberately do not claim to diagnose an unseen manuscript. They teach the Second Pair method, improve the author's question, and route serious uncertainty toward manuscript-level assessment.

## Voice
Copy was revised toward the established Second Pair voice standard: experienced, observant, direct, human and qualified rather than corporate or artificially casual.
