# Second Pair Editorial — Sample Read Protocol

## Purpose

The sample read is a bounded demonstration of how Second Pair works. It should be useful to the author without becoming an unpaid version of the full service.

**Scope:** up to 3,000 words. The author should provide a representative passage and the question they most want investigated. The sample is not a full manuscript diagnosis, developmental edit, line edit, or proofread.

## Intake principle

Record:

- author's stated concern
- manuscript stage
- genre
- sample location
- requested service
- anything the author specifically wants noticed

Do not assume the selected service is correct. It is a starting point.

## During reading

Capture three kinds of notes:

### Reader reaction
What happened to attention, curiosity, emotion or understanding?

### Page evidence
What caused the reaction?

### Pattern signal
Is this likely isolated or does it suggest a larger issue?

## Sample response structure

1. **What worked for me** — one or two specific observations.
2. **Where I hesitated** — concrete page-based examples.
3. **What I think may be behind it** — only where the sample supports it.
4. **What I would look at next** — a short indication, not a full manuscript diagnosis.
5. **Possible next step** — service recommendation only if justified.

## Do not

- rewrite the sample for the author
- dump dozens of line edits into the sample
- diagnose the entire manuscript from two pages
- promise a result
- invent urgency
- imply the author must book a paid service

## What success looks like

The author should finish the sample thinking:

- “They actually read this.”
- “They noticed something I had not seen.”
- “I understand why they reacted that way.”
- “I can see what the next step might be.”

Those reactions are more valuable than trying to impress the author with a large number of comments.
