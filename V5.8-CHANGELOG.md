# V5.8 — Client Journey

This release connects the corpus-informed editorial knowledge system to the practical client journey.

The business now has documented operating guidance for:

- inquiry review
- sample handling
- service routing
- scope and quoting
- booking handoff
- delivery
- client feedback
- follow-up

The website sample request now asks two useful operational questions (start window and discovery source) and explains what happens after submission.

No new pricing, guarantees, testimonials or turnaround promises were added.
